POLICY

Impartiality Policy

How we keep our advice objective and independent of certification outcomes.

1

Commitment

Tango ISO Solutions provides consultancy, documentation and training. We act objectively and base our recommendations on the client's requirements, the applicable standards and legal obligations.

2

Independence from certification

We do not grant ISO certification. Certification is decided solely by independent certification bodies. We never promise, guarantee or influence a certification outcome.

3

Conflicts of interest

Staff must declare any financial, personal or professional interest that could affect their objectivity. Management reviews each declaration and, where necessary, removes the person from the engagement.

4

Commercial pressure

Our fees and scope of work are not conditional on a client's certification result. Staff may not accept gifts or inducements that could influence their judgement.

5

Review

Top management reviews this policy at least once a year and communicates it to all employees.

Confidentiality Policy

How we protect information shared with us by clients, applicants and partners.

1

Scope

This policy covers all information we receive from clients, job applicants and partners, in any form, including documents, records, drawings, data and conversations.

2

Our obligations

We use client information only to deliver the agreed service. Access is limited to staff who need it, and all staff are bound by confidentiality obligations.

3

Disclosure

We do not disclose confidential information to third parties without written consent, except where required by law or a regulatory authority. The client is informed unless the law prevents it.

4

Storage and return

Client files are stored in access-controlled systems. On completion of the engagement, files are returned or securely deleted on request or after the agreed retention period.

5

Breaches

Any suspected breach must be reported to management immediately, investigated, and communicated to affected parties.

Privacy Policy

What personal data we collect through this website and how we use and protect it.

1

What we collect

Through the inquiry and career forms we collect your name, email, phone number, company, message and, for job applications, your resume. We do not sell personal data.

2

Why we collect it

We use this information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotations, deliver services and consider job applications, based on your consent and legitimate business need, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

3

Who processes it

We use trusted service providers to host the website, store files and deliver form messages. They process data only on our instructions.

4

How long we keep it

We keep data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as the law requires, and then delete it.

5

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to tangoisosolutions@gmail.com.

6

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available on this page.
